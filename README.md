# Mehdi Mobtahij

I work in an accounting firm while studying for the DSCG.
When something takes me too long, at work or at home, I usually end up building a tool for it. Here are a few.

### [Year-end closing file](https://github.com/medmehdimobtahij-ship-it/dossier-bilan-web)

A web app to prepare a client's year-end closing: review by cycle, revenue and VAT tie-out, corporate tax, the AGM.
It won't let you close the file while something doesn't add up.

### [CRM for an accounting firm](https://github.com/medmehdimobtahij-ship-it/crm-cabinet-comptable)

Prospects, engagement letters, Outlook and Pennylane in one place, so a new client doesn't get lost between emails and Word templates.

### [VAT tracker](https://github.com/medmehdimobtahij-ship-it/suivi-tva)

Every month, which clients have to file which VAT return, by when, and who on the team is on it. Two people can work on the same month without overwriting each other.

### [CFE notices](https://github.com/medmehdimobtahij-ship-it/avis-cfe)

Every autumn there are dozens of CFE tax notices to send out. This picks the right email for each client depending on how they pay, keeps track of replies and files the PDFs.

### [Bank feed reminders](https://github.com/medmehdimobtahij-ship-it/relance-bancaire)

When a client's bank connection breaks in Pennylane, their books stop updating. This finds them and sends the reconnection emails, one per contact even when they have several companies.

### [Annual accounts email](https://github.com/medmehdimobtahij-ship-it/mail-comptes-annuels)

Drafts the email that sends the annual accounts to each client, pre-filled from Pennylane, and refuses to write it while a required figure is missing.

### [Uber Eats and Deliveroo sales](https://github.com/medmehdimobtahij-ship-it/ventes-plateformes)

Turns the platforms' sales exports into a checked monthly sales entry, with VAT split by rate, ready for Pennylane.

### [Patrimoine](https://github.com/medmehdimobtahij-ship-it/patrimoine)

My bank accounts, savings and crypto in one app, and where the money goes each month. I use it every day. There's a [demo](https://medmehdimobtahij-ship-it.github.io/patrimoine/).

### [CROUS alert](https://github.com/medmehdimobtahij-ship-it/alerte-crous)

A small bot that watches for student housing and pings my phone when something frees up, because refreshing the page all day wasn't working.
